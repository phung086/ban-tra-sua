# World foundation — authorized refinement

Mode: Experience while walking; Operate in the notebook and shop service.
Keep Hanoi, the tea-shop center, authored stories, existing saves, rose palette and Be Vietnam Pro. The user asked to strengthen the visual and UI/UX base before adding more gameplay. No new quests or economy rules in this pass.

## TYPE
Be Vietnam Pro with Vietnamese glyphs. Primary actions remain legible and at least 44px. Short labels and SVG icons replace oversized interface chrome on the scene.

## MATERIAL
Original procedural 3D stays interactive. Warm sun, cool sky fill, distinct foliage and blue lake highlights, contact shadows and readable storefronts should give the scene depth. Shared geometry/materials and an adaptive resolution budget precede expensive effects.

## GROUND
Warm pink paper for the controls; natural street and object colors. Opaque small HUD panels retain contrast. No fullscreen glass or decorative dashboard background.

## THESIS
The visitor feels inside a Hanoi neighborhood connected to their tea shop. A compact notebook organizes existing activities while the street owns the viewport. Graphics choices affect presentation and preserve gameplay.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
On exploration: avatar/street leads, location and energy at upper left, map and camera/return/settings controls at upper right, round stick lower left, contextual action beside it, collapsible notebook at lower edge (right on desktop). In the shop: exposed tools and a compact preparation panel with an obvious open-shop action. Signature interaction: open the notebook to choose a physical destination, collapse it, and follow the avatar through the same world.

## Quality bar and evidence
Code-led interactive 3D refinement, no approved raster comp. The incumbent is the quality reference; do not claim photorealism or Play Together asset parity. The technical bar is both engines producing a playable scene, only one main GPU renderer at a time, preserved position when switching, safe disposal and meaningful adaptive-quality tests. Compare frame submissions at the same shop view before/after; browser samples are not physical-phone benchmarks.
