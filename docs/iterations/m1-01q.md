## Scoped change

In `scripts/m1-browser-benchmark.mjs`, after real pointer drag, wait until the actual follow camera x/z matches the yaw/pitch-derived position relative to `cameraFocus` within 0.035 world units; discard motion frames, wait for >=3 new rendered frames, record cameraFocus, and assert the intended left/right/center yaw. Missing or stale poses must fail rather than produce an image marked as verified. This changes **benchmark instrumentation only**, not gameplay, scene geometry, collision, NPC, or delivery logic.
